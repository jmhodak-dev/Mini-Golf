WILD PUTT — PLAY ON YOUR PC AND PHONE

WINDOWS
1. Install the LTS version of Node.js from https://nodejs.org (22 or newer).
2. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
3. Double-click start-windows.cmd. The first run downloads the server packages.
4. Keep the window open. It prints the Wi-Fi/Ethernet address of your PC.
5. On a phone connected to the SAME Wi-Fi, open that address in Safari or Chrome.
6. Choose Play with friends, create a room, and join the room from other phones.

If Windows Firewall asks, allow Node.js on your PRIVATE/HOME network.
If a phone cannot connect, check that it uses the same home Wi-Fi, not mobile
network or a guest Wi-Fi that isolates devices. The PC must remain awake.

MAC
Install Node.js LTS, extract the ZIP, open Terminal in the extracted folder,
and run: bash start-mac.sh
Then follow the phone steps above.

PLAY JUST ON A COMPUTER, WITHOUT INSTALLING NODE
Download the separate Wild-Putt.html file and open it in a modern browser.
It contains the complete 3D game and supports 1–4 players sharing one device.
Separate-device multiplayer requires the PC-hosted or Render-hosted server.

PHONE APP INSTALLATION
The home Wi-Fi address supports browser play and multiplayer. Reliable offline
installation as a phone app requires HTTPS hosting (for example Render).
Do not forward router ports just to play at home; it is not needed.

GOLF FOR LITTLE PEOPLE
Little Golfer mode is the default: big cups, gentle near-miss help, free resets,
wide bridges, and no time/stroke limits. Stars are optional. Tap the characters!
